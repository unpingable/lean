## Exact bridges and local laws

### Governed Transport

GT separates `Span`, exact fibers, source certificate lift, translation, and
target-local reliance. Source evidence plus route and translation does not
itself authorize target reliance. Negative transport retains source
provenance and remains image-relative until exact target coverage is supplied.
Composition uses compatible pullback witnesses; identity and associativity are
proved through leg-preserving witness equivalence. Exact repair requires an
injective old-route embedding and a genuinely new route. `LAW-FAMILY-ONLY`
remains binding.

### Execution Custody

The exact ladder is:

```text
TicketFresh → MayAttempt
MayAttempt + LocalPreconditions → MayCommit
MayCommit + TicketConsumed + commitSent → CommitAttempted
CommitAttempted + outcome=succeeded → DidExecute
CommitAttempted + outcome=refused → DidNotExecute
CommitAttempted + outcome=unknown → CommitUnknown
DidExecute + safetyWitness → PreservedSafety
obligationReceipt → DischargedObligation
```

The source proves every neighboring non-collapse required by its release and
proves that `CommitUnknown` testifies to neither execution nor non-execution.
It does not bind the asserted outcome to an actuator, model reconciliation,
retain a receipt identity, or prove trajectory-level ticket linearity.

### Someone Continuity

`step` has seven exact transition constructors; `Reachable` is its reflexive
closure under witnessed steps. Ratified qualification adds primitive and
reachable `AgentId` preservation plus reachability composition. Existing laws
preserve well-formedness, coherence, and packet ownership over the reachable
fragment. Positive own-packet admission and foreign-packet nonreachability are
both inhabited. Raw `step` is intentionally wider than the lawful fragment.

## Commonality classification

### Forced semantic commonality

The three sources force at least:

1. a context-indexed family of evidence-bearing judgments;
2. an oriented bridge schema naming source and target judgment kinds;
3. exact bridge evidence bound to exact source and target contexts;
4. a licensed transport operation that consumes both source evidence and the
   bridge evidence;
5. a distinction between target inhabitation and entitlement obtained through
   that bridge;
6. preservation of local non-implications outside the generic constructor.

### Analogous but not yet common core

- GT target-local reliance, Execution local commit premises, and Someone
  standing are all additional gates, but they are not one shared authority
  concept.
- GT typed negative evidence and Execution refusal/unknown judgments have
  substantive structure; Someone has proposition-level negatives only. A
  universal refusal carrier is not yet forced.
- GT and Someone compose routes. Execution Custody deliberately provides no
  generic composition. Composition cannot enter PJ-1 as a core law.
- Execution ticket spend and Someone packet ownership are both conservation
  shaped, but their laws differ. Neither forces a generic spend law.

### Local-only structure

- GT coverage, gaps, exact repair, federation, and pullback coherence;
- Execution ticket lifecycle, actuator flags, outcomes, safety, and discharge;
- Someone scopes, surfaces, classroom, quorum, provenance, substrate strings,
  and retirement projection;
- every institutional owner, revision, epoch, operational custody, runtime
  correspondence, and universal frontier notion.
