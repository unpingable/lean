# PJ Tranche C-prime Context-Transport Classification

Date: 2026-07-22

## Candidate classification

`CONTEXT-TRANSPORT-NOT-GENERIC`

The ratified PJ-A signature indexes sources and targets but defines no
independent operation that transports an existing qualification or receipt to
a new context. The primary calculi do not force one common such operation.

## Per-calculus findings

### Governed Transport

Changing a GT route, span, or coverage region changes the governed bridge
evidence to be established. Exact artifact and translator revisions are
record-bound source qualifications, not context transports modeled by the
formal C-prime witness. Neither kind supplies a generic map carrying an old
exact receipt into a new context. Endpoint equality alone does not preserve
route identity or force.

### Execution Custody

The exact B-prime execution receipt requires literal source/target stage
identity. Two stages with different prestates cannot share that receipt even
if the target predicate is inhabited at both indices. The bounded source
exposes no trajectory or historical/current relation between those stages, so
C-prime does not describe one as temporally succeeding the other.

### Someone Continuity

`Reachable` is the native transition receipt, not a separate context map.
Reachability composes locally and preserves `AgentId` one way, but a
cross-`AgentId` endpoint is not repaired by transporting an old proof. Live
standing is a distinct qualified-source boundary, not an operation modeled by
the C-prime context witness. The qualified surface does not supply an
independent transport of continuity entitlement.

### StaticRole held-out boundary

StaticRole does prove preservation and reflection under its explicit
full-signature and uptake-layer isomorphisms. Those isomorphisms enumerate
the sorts, operations, and coherence laws that must commute. This is local,
strong structure; it is evidence against replacing it with a thin generic PJ
context map.

## Noncommutation boundary

The local hostile results establish the operationally important shape:
independently true target facts may coexist at two indices while
source-relative entitlement does not cross between them. Revision drift,
distinct prestates,
foreign identity, and representational change are different reasons. C-prime
does not weaken their target judgments until a generic square commutes.

## Result

conditional-transport facts, with their distinct hypotheses left intact.
