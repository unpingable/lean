# PJ Tranche C-prime Ownership Classification

Date: 2026-07-22

## Candidate classification

`NO-USEFUL-OWNERSHIP-COMMONALITY`

The exact frozen source surfaces do not jointly expose a stateful institution
entitled to realize a bridge. Adding an `Owner` carrier would therefore name a
vacancy rather than capture a common law.

## Per-calculus findings

### Governed Transport

GT supplies proof-relevant route, translation, reliance, coverage, and
qualification evidence. Those objects describe exact governed crossings, but
the frozen core contains no mutable institution that owns realization. Exact
route evidence is reusable proof; reuse does not perform an operational
crossing.

### Execution Custody

The bounded calculus records a complete asserted stage: permission, send,
ticket state, outcome lane, and discharge facts. Its formal source explicitly
abstracts the actuator and does not provide a multi-stage trajectory or a
linear owner semantics. Ticket consumption is an exact local state fact, not
enough to derive a generic institution over PJ.

### Someone Continuity

Someone supplies events, a step relation, and reachable-fragment evidence.
Events do not identify an institution entitled to enact them. Its hostile
breach sequence permits the same packet to be submitted after demotion; the
qualified source therefore does not contain a durable revocation or restart
owner. `OwnsPacket` is an indexed state property, not bridge ownership.

### StaticRole held-out boundary

StaticRole supplies lawful reference actions, presentation wiring, and
functional discrimination without an institutional prestate or realizing
owner. It is a direct counterexample to making stateful ownership mandatory
in the PJ core.

## Constructive boundary

`PJ/TrancheCPrime/Ownership.lean` demonstrates that PJ entitlements are
ordinary reusable evidence and that the B-prime consumer is pure. Native
examples retain their exact absent-route, non-execution, packet-reuse, and
R2-without-R3 boundaries. Proof reuse is not evidence that an effect occurred
twice, nor is it a modeled history receipt. It shows only that the common PJ
surface has no linear state transition owned by consumption.

## Claims not earned

C-prime does not establish:

- a generic institution, owner, authority, actuator, or state machine;
- one-use conservation or restart reconstruction;
- durable revocation across the primary calculi;
- that bridge descriptions realize themselves;
- that GT qualification, Execution Custody state, and Someone reachability
  are three versions of the same ownership semantics.
