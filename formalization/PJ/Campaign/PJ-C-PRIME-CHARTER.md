ratified at commit `06856876f7f111ad49a17b6eecba5b46ae238211`.

## Method

The campaign may add constructive local boundary theorems and exact instance
analyses. It may not add a generic owner, state, authority, context-transport,
frontier, debt, residue, or obligation carrier merely to make the source
calculi look uniform.

For each proposed common structure the audit asks:

- which independent sources force it;
- whether the same laws hold in those sources;
- whether it adds content beyond a label or record projection;
- whether the held-out calculus refutes its necessity;
- which local distinctions it would erase.

## Success and stop boundary

C-prime succeeds when ownership, context transport, and local residual
structures are classified exactly, including a negative classification. A
successful result need not add a generic theorem. The tranche stops at
`READY-FOR-PJ-C-PRIME-RATIFICATION`; it does not enter the final hostile audit
or choose Planet, Archipelago, Atlas, or Mirage.

integration, operational Continuity correspondence, public aggregate, theory
name, release, tag, mint, publication, or push is authorized.
